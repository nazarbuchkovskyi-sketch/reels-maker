# Real explosive hooks for Reels & TikTok (top-creator level)

A hook is the first 2 seconds that jolt the viewer and make their thumb freeze above the screen.
If the first sentence has no tension, pain, contrast or hard result - **THERE IS NO HOOK**.

All hooks are written **only as direct, energetic words to be said out loud** (no brackets, no directions, hyphen `-` only).

**Language:** write hooks in the user's chosen language (`## Language` in `profile.md`). The examples below are in English only to show the formula and the energy - build a native hook in the user's language, do not translate them word for word.

---

## ⛔ WHAT IS DEFINITELY NOT A HOOK (TYPICAL AI DEFECTS):
- ❌ "You can spend a week preparing a video and get two hundred views. Or you can film it on your phone..." - **THAT IS NOT A HOOK!** That's slow old-man musing on a park bench.
- ❌ "Sometimes your videos just don't get watched..." - smearing porridge around.
- ❌ "Every one of us has faced the fact that..." - boring.
- ❌ "Most people think that..." - an academic lecture.

---

## ✅ GOLDEN FORMULAS FOR REAL HOOKS:

### 1. Hard result contrast (Benchmark #1):
- "Here's the main reason none of your Reels take off, while my videos casually pull views like these."
- "Same video: here two hundred views, here four hundred thousand. The difference is one phrase at the start."
- "While you wait for the algorithm to love your videos, my DMs are flooded with leads like this every day."

### 2. A direct punch into pain and wasted effort (self-recognition):
- "You spend half a day on a Reel, polish the edit - and people just swipe away in the first second."
- "You sit over a video until midnight, post it - and get zero views and zero leads."
- "You spend a fortune on ads and lighting, and people leave before your first point."

### 3. The cost of one specific mistake:
- "One word at the start of your video drains eighty percent of your views before you even open your mouth."
- "If your video starts with a greeting - the algorithm buries it at the very bottom."
- "This one mistake at the start costs you thousands of followers every week."

### 4. Hard truth, no excuses:
- "Your Reels aren't ignored because of the algorithm or a shadowban. It's that your first two seconds are plain water."
- "Here's where eighty percent of your videos actually die before you get to the point."
- "You can film on a two-thousand-dollar camera, but if you open with a slow build-up - nobody watches to the end."

### 5. Cognitive dissonance (shock fact):
- "I stopped planning content a month ahead - and tripled my leads for the first time."
- "I deleted all my long preachy videos, and the account immediately started growing like crazy."
- "Trends and fancy editing almost killed my reach. Here's why."

---

## HOOK CHECK RULE:
Read the first sentence out loud. Does it have a nerve? Does it hit a specific viewer problem?
If it sounds sluggish, vague, or starts with "You can...", "Sometimes..." (or their equivalents in the user's language) - rewrite it! A hook must be short, sharp and merciless.
