# Anti-slop: how to completely clean AI garbage out of Reels & TikTok

**Language:** these rules apply to whatever language the user chose (`## Language` in `profile.md`). The banned phrases below are examples - also ban their natural equivalents in the user's language, including that language's bureaucratic and textbook phrasing.

AI constantly slides into three kinds of defects:
1. **Slow philosophical musings instead of a hook:** "You can spend a week preparing a video... Or you can..." - THAT IS NOT A HOOK! It's vague porridge.
2. **Academic boredom:** "Most people think that... In reality..."
3. **Cheap toxic info-business:** "look", "okay so", "couldn't care less".

---

## 1. STRICT BAN ON IMPERSONAL "MUSINGS" IN THE HOOK

❌ **No slow parables and comparisons at the start:**
- Forbidden: "You can spend a week preparing a video and get two hundred views. Or you can film it on your phone in ten minutes..." - **THAT IS NOT A HOOK!** The viewer swipes on the word "You can".
- Forbidden: "Sometimes it happens that...", "Some people do it one way, others another...", "We often face...".
- Forbidden: "Most people think that... In reality...".

*How to write a real hook (an instant punch to the nerve):*
- "Here's the main reason none of your Reels take off, while my videos casually pull views like these."
- "You spend half a day on a Reel, polish the edit - and people just swipe away in the first second."
- "One word at the start of your video drains eighty percent of your views."

---

## 2. BAN ON SCRIPTWRITER META-TERMINOLOGY ON CAMERA

❌ **The viewer didn't come for a copywriting lecture:**
- Do not put phrases like "Next comes the hook-reason-solution combo", "Here's the right CTA", "This will boost retention" into the script.
- The script must **apply** these principles, not name them out loud!

---

## 3. CHEAP SMM-MENTOR SYNDROME (TOXIC PREACHING)

❌ **No aggressive mentoring or lecturing:**
- Forbidden belittling phrases: "the viewer couldn't care less about you", "you're your own worst enemy", "get ready, this will hurt".
- Forbidden filler openers: "Look", "Okay so", "Listen up".
- Forbidden childish banal lessons: "One video - one idea. Don't cram everything you know into 30 seconds".

*How to write:*
Calmly, confidently and with respect:
"People don't swipe a video because the topic is bad, but because the first two seconds gave them no reason to stay. When a video opens with a build-up or a greeting, the algorithm records a quick swipe and buries the video."

---

## 4. STRICT BAN ON DASHES ("—" AND "–")

❌ **EVERYWHERE, ONLY A HYPHEN ("-") INSTEAD OF A DASH:**
- No em dashes or en dashes ("—", "–") of any kind, in any language.
- Only the short hyphen `-` is used everywhere.

---

## 5. STRICT BAN ON STAGE DIRECTIONS AND BRACKETS

❌ **Writing directions in square brackets is STRICTLY FORBIDDEN:**
- None of: `[Show phone screen]`, `[scroll through numbers]`, `[zoom]`.
- A script is **ONLY THE WORDS** the author says out loud to the camera. Teleprompter text.

---

## 6. STRICTLY FORBIDDEN CHILDISH ANALOGIES AND CLICHÉS

❌ **No everyday metaphors:**
- "It's like asking a stranger to pick a present for your mom: they'll bring a vanilla candle..." - **CRINGE!**
- "It's like cooking soup without the main ingredient..."
- "...and a like from your mom" / "a hundred views and a like from your mom and your cat".
- "Send this to someone who started their video with 'Hi' again".

---

## 7. FORBIDDEN QUESTIONS BEFORE THE SCRIPT

❌ **STOPPING the person with a question is STRICTLY FORBIDDEN:**
"Is there a real story, number or client question on this topic? If not, write 'no story'" - **DO NOT ASK!**
The person gave an idea - you **IMMEDIATELY write a ready, explosive script**.

---

## 8. THE TONE OF A REAL PRO (BENCHMARK, English; block labels go in the user's language)

```
Hook:
Here's the main reason none of your Reels take off, while my videos casually pull views like these.

Part 1:
You spend half a day on a video, set up the lighting, polish the edit. You post it - and get three hundred views. And you don't get why. The person just swiped you away in the first second, because you opened with a long build-up and a greeting. Instagram sees the quick exit and stops showing the video to new people.

Part 2:
Your first sentence has to be about the viewer, not about you. Not "I'm an expert with years of experience", but a specific situation that's annoying them right now. When people hear their own pain, they stay to find out the fix. The faster you give a practical answer without a slow build-up and extra words, the higher the retention and the more reach the algorithm pours in.

Ending:
Your expertise only starts working once someone has listened to the end. Comment "HOOK" - I'll send you the first-line formulas my videos take off with.
```
